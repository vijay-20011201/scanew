Optimized for Empty Containers

The SCA Empty Container ReachStacker is specifically designed and optimized for the efficient handling of empty containers in ports, terminals, and inland depots. Unlike standard reach stackers, this model offers a lighter, more agile platform that delivers significant fuel and operational cost savings.

With a rated capacity of 10 tonnes — perfectly matched for empty container weights — this machine offers the full reach and stacking capability of a standard reach stacker in a more compact and efficient package.

The lighter operating weight reduces ground pressure requirements, extends tire life, and enables operation on surfaces that may not support heavier machines. This makes it ideal for temporary storage areas and facilities with limited ground preparation.
