We SCA Heavy Equipment Pvt Ltd, an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, established as a private limited company in 2013 with its headquarters and manufacturing plants in Bangalore, Karnataka, India.

The Company has indigenously designed and developed a host of material handling equipment like Loaded & Empty Reach stackers, Side Lift Trucks, Various Capacity Forklifts ranging from 10 to 50 tonnes, Various Attachments, to make Material Handling Versatile and Aircraft Push-Back Tractors.

SCA is the first Indian company to indigenously develop and manufacture 45 and 50 ton capacity Forklift trucks, equivalent to the renowned international manufacturers of Material Handling Equipment. These forklift trucks have been supplied to reputed customers like Adani and Goel Pipes.

A total of 300+ Units of Material Handling Equipment are successfully deployed by both vital private & public sector companies, spread over the length & breadth of the country.

After having attained the above milestone, SCA leaped to the manufacture of Electric Reachstackers. SCA is the first Indian Manufacturer to launch Electric Reachstackers in the Indian market with Indigenous content. In a short span of 12 months the company has secured orders for 12 numbers of Electric Reachstackers from esteemed CFS/Port Operators like Gateway Distriparks and J M Baxi Group. The orders received also includes 5 units from Container Corporation of India (CONCOR) A Public Sector Undertaking.

The In-house manufacture of Electric Reachstacker is a significant step forward in supporting MAKE IN INDIA initiative and contributing to the reduction of Carbon footprint across ports and logistics sector. It is estimated that approximately 280 tons of CO2 per RST per year could be reduced by deployment of Electric Reachstacker v/s Diesel Reachstacker.

SCA has established service facilities at various locations in the country to effectively render after-market support to customers. Essential spare parts are also made available along with service to customers.

By undertaking O and M contracts from a variety of customers in the public and private sectors, SCA has also made a strong entry into the service industry.

SCA also offers Material Handling Equipment on Rental basis.
