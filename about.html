Who We Are

SCA Heavy Equipment Pvt Ltd is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company established in 2013 with its headquarters in Bengaluru. With a strong foundation in engineering excellence, SCA specializes in advanced material handling equipment, forklift solutions, container handling systems and heavy-duty lifting machines.

SCA is the first Indian company to introduce electrical reach stackers and battery-operated forklifts. It has successfully supplied 11 units of Electric Reach Stackers (ERST) across India.

The Company has indigenously designed and developed a host of material handling equipment like Loaded & Empty Reach stackers, Side Lift Trucks, Various Capacity Forklifts ranging from 10 to 50 tonnes, Various Attachments (To make Material Handling Versatile) and Aircraft Push-Back Tractors.

More than 300 units, including reach stackers, forklifts, and other equipment, have been successfully sold to leading private and public sector organizations across the length and breadth of the country.

After having attained the above milestone, the company has now added certain additional activities and also some additional equipment in the product portfolio. During the financial year 2024-2025, in addition to the existing operation and maintenance services for all make & models of material handling equipment, the company has also entered into the Trade of Rental Services for all models of equipment manufactured by SCA.
