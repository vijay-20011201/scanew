Power for Ground Operations

SCA Pushback Tractors are engineered for the demanding world of aircraft ground handling and heavy industrial towing operations. These powerful machines are designed to move aircraft weighing over 100 tonnes with precision and safety.

Built on a heavy-duty chassis with 4-wheel drive capability, our pushback tractors deliver exceptional traction and pushing force on ramp surfaces, aprons, and industrial floors. The low-profile design ensures safe operation under aircraft fuselages.

Available in diesel and electric variants, SCA pushback tractors combine raw power with advanced control systems, ergonomic operator cabins, and comprehensive safety features for airport and industrial environments.
