Product Overview

The SCA LT-10 is a heavy-duty 10-ton forklift designed for demanding industrial applications. Built with superior engineering and premium materials, this forklift delivers exceptional performance, reliability, and safety in material handling operations.

Equipped with advanced hydraulic systems and a powerful engine, the LT-10 ensures smooth operation even under maximum load conditions. The ergonomic operator cabin provides comfort during extended shifts, while comprehensive safety features protect both the operator and your valuable cargo.

Whether you're working in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, ports, or construction sites, the LT-10 provides the strength, stability, and precision you need to maximize productivity and operational efficiency.
