Product Overview

The SCA LT-10 is a heavy-duty 10-ton forklift designed for demanding industrial applications. Built with superior engineering and premium materials, this forklift delivers exceptional performance, reliability, and safety in material handling operations.

Equipped with advanced hydraulic systems and a powerful engine, the LT-10 ensures smooth operation even under maximum load conditions. The ergonomic operator cabin provides comfort during extended shifts, while comprehensive safety features protect both the operator and your valuable cargo.

Whether you're working in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, ports, or construction sites, the LT-10 provides the strength, stability, and precision you need to maximize productivity and operational efficiency.

Design Features

Robust Frame Construction: Heavy-duty steel frame engineered to withstand extreme loads and harsh operating conditions while maintaining structural integrity over years of intensive use.

Advanced Hydraulic System: Precision-engineered hydraulic system provides smooth lifting and lowering operations with excellent load control and minimal maintenance requirements.

Ergonomic Operator Cabin: Spacious, climate-controlled cabin with adjustable seating, excellent visibility, intuitive controls, and reduced noise levels for operator comfort during extended shifts.

Safety Systems: Comprehensive safety features including overhead guard, load backrest, emergency stop systems, safety lights, audible alarms, and stability monitoring.

Efficient Engine: High-performance diesel or electric engine options delivering optimal power-to-weight ratio with reduced fuel consumption and lower emissions.

Premium Components: Quality components from trusted global suppliers ensure reliability, longevity, and easy availability of spare parts for minimal downtime.

Versatile Attachments: Compatible with various fork attachments and accessories including side shifters, rotators, clamps, and specialized forks for diverse material handling requirements.
